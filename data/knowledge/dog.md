# Dog care guide

## Walks and exercise
Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of walking or active play every day, split into two sessions (morning and evening). High-energy breeds such as beagles, border collies and huskies may need 60 to 120 minutes. Puppies need shorter, more frequent walks of 10 to 20 minutes. Senior dogs benefit from gentle 15 to 30 minute walks.

## Feeding
Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies under six months need three to four small meals daily. Feeding takes about 10 minutes including fresh water refill. Keep feeding times consistent.

## Grooming
Brush short-haired dogs weekly and long-haired dogs daily or every other day, 10 to 15 minutes. Bathe roughly every 4 to 8 weeks. Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks. Brush teeth daily or at least several times a week, 3 to 5 minutes.

## Training and enrichment
Short training sessions of 5 to 15 minutes once or twice a day keep dogs mentally engaged. Puzzle feeders and sniff games add enrichment.

## Medication and vet care
Give medication at the same time each day as prescribed by a vet. Heartworm and flea preventives are typically monthly (weekly check of reminders is wise). Annual vet checkups are recommended; schedule a 60 minute vet visit.
