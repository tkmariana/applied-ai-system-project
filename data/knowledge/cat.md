# Cat care guide

## Play and exercise
Cats benefit from two to three interactive play sessions per day of 10 to 15 minutes each, using wand toys or laser pointers. Kittens and young cats need more play. Play before meals mimics hunting.

## Feeding
Adult cats are usually fed two meals a day; kittens need three to four. Feeding takes about 5 to 10 minutes including refreshing water. Wet food portions should be measured to avoid obesity.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once daily (5 minutes) and fully change litter and wash the box weekly (15 minutes).

## Grooming
Brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats daily, 5 to 10 minutes. Trim claws every 2 to 3 weeks. Dental care such as tooth brushing a few times a week takes 3 minutes.

## Medication and vet care
Give medication at consistent times as prescribed by a vet. Annual vet checkups are recommended; plan about 60 minutes including travel. Flea preventive is usually monthly.
