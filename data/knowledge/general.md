# General pet care and scheduling tips

## Routine
Consistent daily routines reduce stress in pets. Schedule feeding, exercise and medication at the same times each day. Place high-priority tasks such as medication and feeding first, then exercise, then grooming and enrichment.

## Safety and veterinary advice
PawPal suggestions are general guidance and not veterinary advice. If a pet shows signs of illness, injury, vomiting, lethargy or loss of appetite, contact a veterinarian. Never change medication doses without a vet.

## Time budgeting
Group short tasks together. Leave a buffer of 10 minutes between tasks. Schedule exercise before meals for dogs and play before meals for cats. Avoid heavy exercise right after eating.

## Water
Always keep fresh water available. Refill water bowls at least once a day.
