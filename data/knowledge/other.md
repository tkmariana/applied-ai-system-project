# Small pets and other animals care guide

## Rabbits and guinea pigs
Provide unlimited hay and fresh water daily. Offer fresh vegetables once a day (5 minutes). Spot-clean the cage daily (10 minutes) and do a full clean weekly (30 minutes). Rabbits need at least 3 hours of exercise outside the cage; handle and socialize 15 to 30 minutes daily.

## Birds
Refresh food and water daily (5 minutes). Provide 1 to 2 hours of out-of-cage time and 15 minutes of interaction. Clean the cage bottom daily and the full cage weekly.

## Hamsters and gerbils
Refill food and water daily (5 minutes). Spot-clean every few days and fully clean weekly. They are nocturnal, so evening interaction of 10 minutes is best.

## Fish
Feed once or twice a day (2 minutes). Test water weekly and change 20 to 25 percent of the water weekly (20 minutes). Clean the filter monthly.

## Reptiles
Feeding schedules vary by species, from daily to every few days. Check heat and humidity levels daily (5 minutes). Clean enclosures weekly (30 minutes).
